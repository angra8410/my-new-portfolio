# Outline: Quarterly Business Review (QBR) - Campaign Performance Analysis
**Project:** Publicis Digital Advertising Optimization  
**Date:** Q4 2021  
**Analyst:** Antonio Gutierrez

---

## 1. Executive Summary (The Big Picture)
*   **Total Reach:** 275M Impressions delivered across DCM.
*   **Engagement:** 669K Total Clicks with an overall CTR of 0.24%.
*   **Site Traffic:** 2.38M Sessions and 111K unique users (GA).
*   **Efficiency:** Identified critical attribution gaps and budget reallocation opportunities to improve total clicks by 40%.

## 2. Analytical Approach & Framework
*   **Data Integration:** Cross-source reconciliation between DCM (Paid Media) and Google Analytics (On-site Behavior).
*   **Methodology:** Layered analysis across:
    *   Placement Performance (Efficiency vs. Volume)
    *   Creative Impact (Format performance)
    *   Device Breakdown (Mobile dominance)
    *   Temporal Trends (Seasonal alignment)

## 3. Placement Efficiency: Volume vs. Performance
*   **The Volume Leaders:** Aptus (77M), Zeta (47M), and MediaIQ (41M) drive the bulk of impressions.
*   **The Efficiency Stars:** 
    *   **Facebook:** 1.23% CTR (5x campaign average).
    *   **Sharecare:** 0.55% CTR.
*   **The Underperformers:** Zeta and MediaIQ consume 32% of the budget with a low 0.08% CTR.

## 4. Consumer Behavior & Device Insights
*   **Mobile Dominance:** 71% of GA sessions originate from mobile devices.
*   **Format Performance:** 320x50 mobile banner is the top-performing creative size.
*   **Seasonality:** Strong upward trend in Sep-Oct (117% increase in impressions) tied to seasonal skin triggers.

## 5. Technical Audit: The Attribution Gap
*   **The Findings:** 54% Click-to-Session rate (Industry benchmark: 70-85%).
*   **The Issues:** 46% of paid clicks are "lost" before reaching GA.
*   **Root Causes:** Landing page latency, broken UTM parameters, or redirect chain issues.
*   **Tracking Artifacts:** "Transparent 1x1" and "(not set)" creatives distending CTR data.

## 6. Strategic Recommendations (Action Plan)
*   **Budget Rebalancing:** Shift 15-20% of spend from Zeta/MediaIQ to Facebook and Sharecare.
*   **Creative Strategy:** Expand mobile creative suite; audit "Dermicool" landing page for Core Web Vitals.
*   **Flighting:** Pre-load August flights by 3-4 weeks to capture early seasonal intent.
*   **Data Quality:** Suppress invalid creative tags and implement server-side GA tagging to close the attribution gap.

## 7. Next Steps & Reporting Roadmap
*   **Live Dashboard:** Real-time monitoring in Power BI.
*   **Weekly Snapshots:** Automated KPI emails to stakeholders.
*   **Attribution Audit:** Deep dive into the top 5 placement redirect chains.
